Questions

Where does the data come from?

USAspending.gov, the official public record of federal spending. Every name and figure in your report comes straight from those award records.

What if you can't find enough firms?

If fewer than 5 firms match your request, you get a full refund automatically, by email.

Do you contact the firms for me?

No. You reach out directly, using the SAM.gov contact link included for each firm.

How fast is delivery?

Orders are checked every 10 minutes, so most reports arrive well within an hour. Check your spam folder if it hasn't arrived.
